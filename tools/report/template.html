Cohort Pulse

Cohort-over-cohort view of the admissions funnel, built from Firestore. Each plot is a candidate for the new HQ report tab. The numbers are real and deduplicated to one record per email per cohort. duplicate records are dropped.

Cohort over cohort

Pacing: cumulative volume counting down to cohort start

This replaces "applications this week." Every cohort is set on the same countdown, from 11 weeks before start (T-11w) to kickoff (T-0). That answers whether the live cohort is on track to fill by day one. A line starts above zero when applications arrived before T-11w. Deferrals carried over from the previous cohort are left out.

Same curves as a share of each cohort's total at kickoff. This shows velocity: how early a cohort fills, not how big it gets.

Signed seats counting down to cohort start

The same countdown for signed enrollment agreements: the count first, then each cohort's share of its kickoff total. This is the funnel's output, so the curve shows how late the roster locks in.

Signed as a share of each cohort's signed total at kickoff

Funnel step conversion by cohort

Each panel is one step of the funnel. The bar shows what percent of the people who reached that step made it to the next one, so the panels chain left to right:

  • Step 1: Applied → took CCAT. Of everyone who applied, the percent who completed the CCAT.
  • Step 2: Took CCAT → accepted. Of CCAT-takers, the percent we accepted (adminDecision accepted or GFA).
  • Step 3: Accepted → signed. Of accepted candidates, the percent who signed the enrollment agreement.
  • Overall: applied → signed. Steps 1 × 2 × 3: the percent of all applicants who became signed students.

Hover a bar for the counts behind it. C4 is left off because it has no decision or contract data in Firestore. C5 acceptances are inferred from status = step-6-approve. C8 is still in progress (faded bars), and its records are created later in the form flow, which inflates its step 1 rate.

CCAT band mix by cohort

Share of CCAT-takers in each policy band. This is the quality of the top of the funnel, and it caps how many acceptances a given volume can produce.

Score distribution. The box is the middle 50% of scores, the tick is the median, the dot is the mean, and the whiskers run from the 10th to the 90th percentile. Grey lines mark the 32 / 35 / 40 cutoffs.

CCAT velocity by application month

Share of applicants who finish the CCAT within 14 days of applying. This is the metric the nurture sequences move.

Month-over-month change, in percentage points

Grouped by createdAt month across all cohorts. The current month is still maturing and is drawn lighter.

Decision SLA: CCAT complete → decision

Days from CCAT completion to the admissions decision. The bar runs from p25 to p75, the tick is the median, and the whisker extends to p90.

Decision time is read from admissionsUpdatedAt, which is the last admissions touch and not a dedicated decision timestamp. It overstates lag wherever a record was touched again after the decision.

Accepted but unsigned, by days since acceptance

This is the pool closest to revenue. It's split by whether a DocuSign envelope has gone out, because a stuck candidate and a contract nobody sent need different fixes.

Age is measured from admissionsUpdatedAt. "Not sent" means no contractEnvelopeId is on the record.
